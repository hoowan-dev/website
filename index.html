Juan Becerra

Juan Becerra is a software engineer with 5+ years in AAA game development, building gameplay, animation, tools, and AI systems for large-scale studio productions. His production tools have helped ship games and been adopted across multiple studios to speed up content creation, and lately he's been building at the intersection of game dev and AI: a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) prototype for finding animations by style, mood, and posture, and ProtoBoy, an MCP server that grounds AI coding agents in a game's design docs. On his own time, he's building Fellowship, a third-person action RPG in Unreal Engine 5, and wrote Hoowan, his own game engine in C++ and OpenGL. He holds a B.S. in Computer Science from Boise State University and is taking graduate coursework in machine learning and deep learning at the University of Washington. He is based in Seattle, WA and is open to remote, on-site, or hybrid roles in AI engineering, game development, and software engineering.

Outside of work, Juan is a gigging musician for several local and touring bands. He enjoys doing session work for recording artists and playing live shows, and has experience in music production.
